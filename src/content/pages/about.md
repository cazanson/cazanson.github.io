## Ca’ Zanson?
In the early-1970’s my US Army father had our family stationed in Vicenza, Italy, 45 miles west from Venice and smack-dab in the middle of Italy’s Veneto region. Venetian pronunciation softens the Italian soft gi/ge (dʒ), such that ‘Gian’ (John) in Venetian sounds like ‘Zan’ in English.

“Ca’ di Zan” means ”John’s house” (or "House of John") in Venetian, so “ca’ Zanson” would mean ”Johnson’s house.” Hmmm. A fun domain name if your given name is a snoozer like mine, right? I snapped it up.

Enjoy your stay.
